What “warm start” means

This file does not falsely claim that a 10M model has been pretrained on a huge internet-scale dataset. It starts with a deterministic English-language warm-start state and a built-in corpus, then lets you continue training locally. For genuinely pretrained weights, replace the warm-start checkpoint with a real checkpoint produced from a substantial corpus.
